# Logo brief

The prompt used to generate the Lens mark. Kept here so the brand can be
regenerated consistently, and so the reasoning behind the constraints is not
lost the next time someone reaches for a camera icon.

Two notes on why this brief says what it says:

**The palette is the product's real dark theme**, converted from the `oklch()`
values in `packages/docs/app/globals.css`. The accent is used in hairline
quantities on purpose: a site about image quality cannot have saturated chrome
sitting next to the sample images, and the same restraint applies to the mark.

**The "constant ratio" idea is not decoration.** Lens spaces its output sizes
geometrically, so each variant is a fixed ratio larger than the one below it.
A mark built on nested forms at a constant ratio encodes the library's actual
behaviour rather than illustrating a camera.

---

Create a premium developer-product logo for Lens, a composable image
optimization library for Node.js: an open-source toolkit that resizes,
compresses and converts images, then hands them to pluggable storage.

The logo should feel like it belongs alongside Linear, Raycast, Vercel, Stripe,
Resend, Clerk and Figma. Premium, confident, engineered. Built for developer
infrastructure, not enterprise software and not a consumer photo app.

The style must be extremely minimal, geometric, sophisticated and timeless.

The icon must NOT contain:
  camera bodies or camera lenses with glass elements
  aperture blades, iris shapes or shutter mechanisms
  shutter buttons or viewfinders
  photographs, polaroid frames or picture frames
  the generic "mountain and sun inside a rectangle" image placeholder
  magnifying glasses
  eyes or optical diagrams with light rays
  film strips, negatives or sprocket holes
  arrows pointing inward to suggest compression
  download, upload or cloud icons
  gears, sliders or toggles
  folders or file icons
  generic checkmarks
  an obvious "L" lettermark

Instead create an abstract symbol that subtly communicates:
  one source becoming a precise set of many
  fidelity kept while weight falls away
  clarity arriving through reduction rather than addition
  proportion and a constant ratio between steps
  something resolving into focus
  precision, measurement and control
  a transformation that is exact rather than lossy in spirit
  restraint, and an engineer's confidence in the result

The symbol should read as one thing resolving into an ordered set, in
proportion. Distillation rather than a device. Structure and ratio rather than
optics.

Explore abstract geometric forms that could subtly suggest:
  nested or concentric forms stepping at one constant ratio
  a single shape shedding weight while keeping its silhouette
  layered planes coming into register
  a set of proportional units reading as one whole
through negative space, layered geometry, offset repetition, or forms that
share an edge. Do not create an obvious literal lettermark.

The icon must be distinctive enough to work as a standalone app icon and be
recognizable without the wordmark. Use smooth curves combined with geometric
precision. Avoid unnecessary detail.

It must work perfectly at 16px as a favicon and 24px as an app icon, while
still looking premium enlarged. At small sizes the nesting must stay legible
rather than collapsing into a solid block, so keep the count of elements low
and the spacing between them generous.

Give it subtle depth through layering, carefully controlled gradients and
dimensional geometry, inspired by Apple's modern iconography, while staying
clean and professional. Not glossy, not 3D, not cartoon-like. Flat enough to be
a serious modern product icon, with just enough depth to feel alive.

WORDMARK

The wordmark reads: lens
Lowercase. A clean geometric sans-serif: Inter, SF Pro Display, Geist or
Circular Std. Inter is the product's actual typeface, so favour it. Letter
spacing should feel premium and carefully considered, slightly tightened.
The typography should feel confident, clean, modern, precise and calm.
The icon and the typography should look like they were designed together
rather than assembled separately.

COLOR PALETTE (the product's real dark theme)

Background: near-black #08090A, the app canvas. Not pure black.
Surface: #0F1012 and #151619, the two elevations above the canvas. Useful for
  the quieter layers inside the mark.
Primary accent: teal-cyan #009EAE. The single point of colour.
Accent bright: #14BBC2, the same hue lifted, for a highlight edge or the one
  element that resolves.
Hairline: #23252A, for structural separation inside the mark if an edge is
  needed. Prefer this over a black outline.
Text and neutral: off-white #F7F8F8 for a wordmark sitting directly on the
  dark background.

The accent is deliberately cyan-teal rather than the blue every developer tool
already uses, and it is used in hairline quantities in the product because
saturated chrome sitting next to sample images changes how those images read.
The mark must inherit that restraint: mostly neutral geometry, with the teal
earning its place as a single resolved element, edge or gradient terminus.

Explore a restrained gradient from #0F1012 through #23252A to #009EAE, with
#14BBC2 as a single bright edge, on the near-black background, without making
the mark busy or overly colourful. One hue only. No second accent colour.

Avoid black outlines. Avoid neon glow. Avoid complex multi-stop gradients.
Avoid rainbow or multicolour treatments entirely, which would undercut a tool
whose job is to preserve colour faithfully.

PERSONALITY

Lens should feel precise, calm, intelligent, modern, confident, durable and
quietly technical. Auditable rather than clever. Effortless rather than
energetic. It should read as a serious, well-engineered piece of infrastructure
that happens to be pleasant to use, not a consumer photo tool and not a
marketing brand.

DELIVER

1. Standalone icon. The symbol only, transparent background.
2. Horizontal logo. Symbol to the left of the lowercase "lens" wordmark,
   transparent background.
3. Monochrome version. Single colour symbol and wordmark, for contexts where
   the teal cannot be used.
4. App icon. The symbol only, optimized for a rounded square safe area, on
   #08090A as its own filled background.

All versions transparent except the app icon.

Clean vector geometry, crisp edges, smooth curves. Must work as an app icon,
favicon, splash screen, in-app branding, npm and GitHub avatar, and social
profile.

No mockups. No devices. No presentation boards. No additional text. No
decorative backgrounds beyond the specified dark canvas on the app icon.

Prioritize originality, simplicity and recognizability over literal
representation of photography or optics. The symbol should be distinctive
enough that over time people see it alone and immediately think of Lens.
